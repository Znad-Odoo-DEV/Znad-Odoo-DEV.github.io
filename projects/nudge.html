Nudge, an AI companion inside Odoo

Press a shortcut on any Odoo screen and ask, in Arabic or English. Nudge explains what you are looking at, does the work with your own rights once you say yes, and builds automations, emails and reports for you.

The problem

Odoo is large, and most people who use it every day know a handful of its screens. When they need anything else they ask a colleague, search the documentation, or wait for an administrator to make a change that would take minutes if anyone had the time.

A general chatbot does not close that gap. It cannot see the record on the screen, does not know how this particular database is configured, and should never be handed write access to a company’s books.

What I built

One shortcut opens Nudge on any screen. It reads what the person is looking at (the record, its fields, unsaved changes, buttons and tabs) only when a message is sent, and only what that person may see. It knows the database it is installed on: which modules are custom, which fields they add, how each app is configured.

  • Ask, in Talk mode. Explains the screen and walks through it with a pointer that goes to the field or button to use, step by step. Answers how Odoo works from the official documentation shipped with the module, 1,067 pages searched in PostgreSQL, with nothing fetched from outside.
  • Do, in Agent mode. Finds, creates and changes records and presses buttons with the person’s own rights, after a card that shows what will happen and waits for a yes. Many records go through one plan: tried first in a savepoint and undone, so the card can say how many will pass and why the others will not, with undo afterwards. Long work runs in the background; scheduled tasks are asked for in words.
  • Build, in Developer mode. Writes automation rules, server actions, designed email templates, fields, views and PDF reports. Each one is tried on a dummy record first, and everything it builds can be undone.

Around the three modes: slash commands that work with no AI at all (/find, /product, /stock, /doc), dictation into any field, @Nudge in the chatter, spoken questions in Arabic and English, and token and cost budgets per person and per company.

It talks to Claude, Gemini, any ChatGPT-compatible API, or a model on the company’s own server through Ollama, for data that must not leave the building. A cheaper model can answer questions while a stronger one does the work.

The result

Eleven modules for Odoo 17, 18 and 19, on Community and Enterprise: the core, the website and shop builder, the website assistant, WhatsApp and Telegram, external assistants, emails, Discuss and spreadsheets. The bridges to Studio, Odoo AI and Documents are Enterprise-only, because the apps they bridge are.

The test suite holds 1,508 Python tests and 285 browser tests on Odoo 19, and is run on 19, 18 and 17 before each release, together with a real update from the previous published version.

Before release it went through a security and store-readiness review. Queries the agent may run are read-only at the level of the database transaction, with an allowlist of functions. Python it writes runs with a deadline, a check of its syntax tree and a read-only view of the records, in a savepoint that is rolled back. What a tool reads from a record is framed as data, so an instruction hidden in a note is not taken as a command, and uploads are capped at 10 MB.

Getting started and everyday use

Everything you need to open Nudge, talk to it in your own language and make it yours from the first day.

The avatar in the corner

Nudge is the small round face at the corner of every Odoo screen. It animates while Nudge works, carries a number for the background requests that ended since you last looked, and can be dragged anywhere.

Steps

  1. Click the round face to open the panel.
  2. Drag it to another place on the screen; it stays there on every page.
  3. In the panel, press “Put it back in the corner” to undo the move.
  4. When a number shows on the face, open the panel: that many background requests have ended.

Result

In the trial, the badge on the avatar lit when a background job finished, and clicking it opened the done card “Set delivery date of Raghad's offers to 2026-10-19 — 16 done”.

Note Nudge: Off in your user menu hides the avatar for you only.
