Electronics repair workshop

A modular app that follows a device from the counter to the customer.

View on GitHub

The problem

A repair workshop needs to follow each device from reception to delivery, without having to adopt every stage on the first day.

What I built

A modular app that follows a device from the counter to the customer: reception, diagnosis, parts, repair, billing, delivery and reporting, each stage its own installable module, so the workshop can adopt them one at a time.

The result

The workshop can adopt the stages one at a time.
